# exocortex

An augmented memory and productivity engine designed to reduce friction, gamify growth, and serve as a modular foundation for personal software infrastructure.

---

## Vision & Philosophy

`exocortex` is built around behavioral psychology and high-performance computing principles:
* **Friction Engineering:** Minimizing activation energy for beneficial habits while replacing empty convenience with productive friction.
* **Invisible Progress Made Visible:** Gamifying study, memorization, and task execution to make personal growth tangible.
* **Radical Ownership:** Slowly shedding third-party abstractions in favor of lean, local-first control.

---

## Objectives

### Functional Capabilities
* **Knowledge & Memorization:** Local-first knowledge storage paired with gamified study systems.
* **Obligation & Schedule Tracking:** Proactive reminders and intelligent scheduling support.
* **Content Generation:** Assisting in drafting and structuring ideas to inspire others.

### Architectural & Engineering Goals
* **Performance:** Making the most of whatever hardware it is running on.
* **Modular Ecosystem:** Serving as an incubation bed for reusable Rust libraries across other projects.
* **Quality Baseline:** Establishing a high engineering standard for code structure, testing, and workflow.
* **Experimental Sandbox:** Maintaining a low-stakes environment for trying new software patterns.

---

## Architecture & Design Principles

* **Language:** Rust (2024 Edition)
* **License:** Dual-licensed under [MIT](LICENSE-MIT) OR [Apache-2.0](LICENSE-APACHE)
* **Storage:** Local, plain-text / structured binary formats with zero external dependencies where practical.
